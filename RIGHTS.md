# Rights and image redistribution

**Public GitHub content:** our source code, original artwork, documentation, and bibliographic/curation metadata.

**Not public here:** paper PDFs, cropped figures, generated thumbnails of paper figures, contact sheets, and the private full-resolution archive. A visually approved crop has passed a quality test, **not** a copyright clearance test.

The [CVF Open Access repository](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/) says copyright remains with authors or other holders. Some journal articles use Creative Commons licenses; the license, figure credit lines, and third-party components must be checked for each figure. Under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), sharing requires attribution, a license link, and indication of changes such as cropping or format conversion. A paper-level license alone should not be assumed to cover every third-party image within a figure.

Future public image releases require, per figure:

- `rights_holder` and `license_id` (or explicit permission reference)
- `rights_evidence_url`, `license_url`, and `verified_at`
- `attribution` and `modifications`
- explicit `redistribution_status: approved`

Only figures that pass both **visual QA** and **rights QA** may be included in public previews, releases, or downloads. Report rights concerns by opening an issue with the figure ID and source URL; do not upload the image in the issue.
