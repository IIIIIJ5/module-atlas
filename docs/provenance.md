# Snapshot and provenance

The full reviewed library has **1,131** diagrams from **609** papers. **1,008** meet the fixed recent window of **2025-11-11 through 2026-11-11**. The public `recent-1000` snapshot selects exactly 1,000 of those 1,008: journal entries first, then compact Intro / comparison / framework / module detail, then newest publication date and stable ID. It contains **561 journal** and **439 conference** entries.

Recent journal entries require a verified online publication date. Official CVPR, ICLR, and ICML 2026 proceedings qualify by their 2026 publication. Other undated 2026 journal entries are not silently counted as recent, even if their year suggests they probably qualify. This conservative rule can be revised when exact dates are documented.

The public catalog preserves source links and crop metadata, but omits local file paths, full figure captions, PDFs, and image binaries. Its `visual_checked` flag means the final exported crop was inspected. It does not mean the public repository may host the image.

The private original archive, paper downloads, review sheets, and unsubmitted reference material are intentionally outside this repository.
